About FEROKINZA

FEROKINZA EXPORT & IMPORT is an Italy-based international sourcing, procurement, and export company connecting businesses worldwide with reliable European products and supply solutions. Our core specialization is European sourcing, working with manufacturers, brands, distributors, and specialized suppliers across different sectors to identify and procure genuine products according to each customer's technical, commercial, and quantity requirements. With an international operating network extending across Europe, the GCC, India, Asia, and Africa, FEROKINZA brings together European sourcing expertise and global market understanding.

Our business model is built around flexible procurement, supporting customers from a single product or spare part to complete project requirements and full-container shipments. Our dedicated procurement teams manage product identification, supplier sourcing, alternative product solutions, quotation, procurement coordination, and international delivery. Whether a customer needs one specialized component or large-volume industrial supplies, we aim to provide the same level of accuracy, responsiveness, reliability, and professional service—because every customer and every order matters.

FEROKINZA works across multiple sectors, including electrical and automation, industrial and mechanical equipment, plumbing and water solutions, construction materials, healthcare and hospital supplies, hospitality products, OEM components, maintenance requirements, and specialized industrial products. Through our developing relationships and collaborations with European brands, manufacturers, and suppliers, together with our established business experience in the GCC, India, Africa, and other international markets, we help customers access products that may otherwise be difficult to source efficiently.

Our long-term vision is to develop FEROKINZA into a global European sourcing and procurement platform where businesses, regardless of their location or order size, can access reliable European products through one trusted partner. We aim to combine technology, international supplier networks, dedicated procurement expertise, and efficient logistics to make global sourcing simpler, faster, and more cost-effective. From one piece to one container, from a single requirement to long-term supply partnerships—FEROKINZA is building a smarter connection between European supply and global demand.

FEROKINZA supports procurement requirements across a broad range of industries and product categories, with particular expertise in sourcing food & beverage products from Europe and trusted suppliers worldwide. Our food procurement capabilities include chocolate, chocolates and confectionery, premium and luxury food brands, beverages, packaged foods, ingredients, snacks, specialty foods, hospitality supplies, and other essential consumer products. Beyond food and beverage, we source construction materials, electrical & automation products, industrial and mechanical equipment, plumbing & sanitary products, water-treatment solutions, healthcare and hospital supplies, hotel and hospitality products, automotive and marine components, oil & gas equipment, manufacturing and packaging products, safety equipment, agricultural products, energy solutions, OEM parts, and specialized project requirements. While European sourcing remains one of our core strengths, FEROKINZA can arrange procurement from trusted sources across Europe and other global markets, supported by practical business and procurement experience across Europe, the GCC, India, Africa, Asia, and the USA, including successfully completed projects for the US market. Our objective is simple: to provide businesses worldwide with reliable access to the products they need—from chocolates, beverages, premium brands, and everyday essentials to specialized industrial requirements—through one professional global procurement partner.
