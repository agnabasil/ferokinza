What we do

European sourcing, handled end to end

Questions

Before you send an enquiry

01Do you accept small orders?

Yes. We support customers from small batches through to complete project requirements and full-container shipments. Small orders are part of the business model, not an exception we tolerate.

02Do you only source from Europe?

European sourcing is our core strength, but we can arrange procurement from trusted sources across Europe and other global markets when that is the better answer for your specification or budget.

03I only have a photo or an old part number. Can you still help?

Yes. Product identification is the first step of our process. Send whatever you have - a nameplate photograph, a drawing, a sample, a catalogue page - and we work back to the manufacturer and current reference.

04What if the item is discontinued or out of budget?

We propose alternative product solutions: an equivalent from another European manufacturer, a superseded-to-current replacement, or a different specification that meets the same requirement. You decide which option to quote against.

05Can you combine several suppliers into one shipment?

Yes. Consolidating goods from multiple European suppliers into a single consignment is one of the main reasons customers use us - one order, one shipment, one set of documents.

06How long does a quotation take?

It depends on the product and how quickly the supplier responds. We acknowledge every enquiry, tell you what additional information we need, and keep you informed while the sourcing is in progress.

07Can you handle large-scale and full-container requirements?

Yes. Complete project lists, BOQ packages, bulk industrial supplies and full-container shipments are all part of the business. Larger requirements are quoted line by line, planned against your delivery schedule and shipped as one coordinated consignment.

08Can you support ongoing supply, not just one-off orders?

Yes. Many customers move from a first enquiry to repeat supply: recurring MRO and spare-part lists, scheduled replenishment and framework pricing on the items you buy regularly. Once your requirements are known, repeat orders are faster to quote and easier to plan.
